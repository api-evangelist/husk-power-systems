# Husk Power Systems

> Energy technology company (founded 2008) that builds, owns and operates AI-enabled distributed
> renewable energy systems — community solar minigrids, commercial and industrial solar, and
> residential rooftop — for unserved and underserved communities, businesses and households across
> Africa and Asia. Headquartered in Fort Collins, Colorado, with operating hubs in Patna, Mumbai and
> Pune (India) and Abuja (Nigeria).

## API status

Husk Power Systems publishes **no public developer program**. As of 2026-08-22 there is no developer
portal, no API reference, no SDK, no webhook documentation, and no machine-readable API description
(OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection or JSON Schema) on any Husk host. Husk's software
reaches the world as end-user and operator products, not as an API product:

- **HUSKify** — customer mobile app for pay-as-you-go energy, bill payment and smart-meter top-up.
- **Husk Neuron** — internal operations dashboard and field-team app for the minigrid fleet.
